Open data

The word lists, definitions, sentences, stroke data and font in Ink & Tone come from open projects. Here they are exactly as the app ships them, free to download, with their licences.

FileWhat it holdsLicenceSize

Sources and changes

ThirdPartyNotices.txt names every source, says what was changed and when, and holds the full licence texts. The adapted CC-CEDICT definitions are released under CC BY-SA 4.0.

How the files were built

build_chars.py, build_strokes.py, build_widget_words.py, build_words.py, clean_meanings.py, credit_sentences.py, rename_font.py

The app's own code is not open source; these licences cover the data and the font only.
